## The problem

When a wildfire is active, knowing where it will spread in the next few hours helps decide where to send resources and who to warn. The data to estimate it exists (satellite-detected hotspots, wind, temperature, humidity), but it is scattered and hard to read.

## The solution

FireSpotter brings NASA's active fire detections and current weather data together on one map and estimates where each hotspot will move. A colour scale shows whether the risk is rising or falling.

We built it during the 48 hours of the NASA Space Apps Challenge, with framework-free JavaScript and Leaflet for the map, using Git and Jira to organise the work.
